WhatisGlaucoma?
Glaucoma is a group of eye conditions that damage the optic nerve, which is vital for good
vision. This damage is typically caused by an abnormally high pressure inside your eye
(intraocularpressure).
Because vision loss caused by glaucoma is irreversible, early diagnosis and consistent
treatmentareessentialtoslowdownorpreventfurtherdamage.
